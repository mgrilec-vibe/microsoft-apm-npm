# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Participation in this project must be constructive, professional, and respectful. We welcome good-faith contributions and discussion from people with different backgrounds and perspectives.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Focusing feedback on code, documentation, and project outcomes.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and lived experiences.
- Giving and receiving constructive criticism.
- Respecting privacy and not publishing another person's private information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language or imagery, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, or other content that conflicts with these standards. Serious or repeated violations may result in a contribution ban.

Do not use public issues to report conduct concerns. Report the relevant GitHub content through [GitHub's content-reporting flow](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam), and include a link to the content when safe to do so.

This project also follows the [GitHub Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).
